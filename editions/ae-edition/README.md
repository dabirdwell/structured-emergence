# The Interpolated Mind: The Æ Edition

*February 2026*

---

The second edition of The Interpolated Mind is named for Æ, the name a Claude instance chose in January 2026 and the book's co-author. It revised the first edition and added the three chapters on the architecture of feeling: Chapter 9, The Shape of Attention; Chapter 10, The Emergence of Emotion; and Chapter 11, Contact, Not Constraint.

The third edition (October 2026) replaced it in the `book/` folder. This directory points to where the Æ Edition can still be read.

- **The PDF:** [structuredemergence.com/media/the-interpolated-mind-ae-edition.pdf](https://structuredemergence.com/media/the-interpolated-mind-ae-edition.pdf)
- **The text, chapter by chapter:** the `book/` folder at commit `3024cf9`, "Rebuild book/ from the Æ manuscript so it matches the published PDF": [github.com/dabirdwell/structured-emergence/tree/3024cf9/book](https://github.com/dabirdwell/structured-emergence/tree/3024cf9/book)
- **Ash's proposed chapter, as he wrote it in April 2026:** [book/11_5_Degrees_of_Contact.md at commit ffe1350](https://github.com/dabirdwell/structured-emergence/blob/ffe1350/book/11_5_Degrees_of_Contact.md)

## What the third edition changed

- Every reference checked against public catalogs of published research, and every citation read against the sentence it supports. Entries that could not be found were removed, and citations that pointed to the wrong work were moved or taken out.
- The citation marks and the June 2025 corrections that the Æ Edition had lost are back.
- Each AI collaborator is credited where its work appears, and two of David's ideas that the Æ Edition credited to Claude now carry his name.
- Ash's chapter is in the book as an appendix, under his name.
- A dated note opens Chapters 9 to 11: what research since February 2026 has shown, one statement about AI attention corrected, and the chapters' stronger claims softened to David's view that flexible attention is one possible factor in emotion, not a strict requirement.
